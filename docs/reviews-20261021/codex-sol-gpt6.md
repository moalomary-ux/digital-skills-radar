### A. Verdict

Review basis: v8 source and the existing `dist` snapshot; the live URL could not be fetched from this environment. I made no changes and ran no build.

- **Product concept — 8/10.** Connecting government work, role capabilities, assessment, and development plans is a strong flagship concept.
- **User journey — 3/10.** The assessment currently has code paths that strand users, while leaders reach information more often than a usable decision.
- **Visual/brand — 6/10.** The palette and typography are coherent, but repeated cards and official entity logos weaken the product’s independent identity.
- **Engineering — 3/10.** A central flow defect, state-only navigation, and a large initial bundle make the current release fragile.
- **Content/evidence — 4/10.** The methodology is unusually candid, yet prominent labels and derived claims still exceed what the underlying records establish.

### B. Top 10 weaknesses, ranked by impact on a senior government audience

1. **The assessment cannot reliably finish.** This defeats the product’s main promise. The employee entry preselects a role, while the wizard initializes at step 1 when a role exists, bypassing the required track choice; later, progression requires track scenarios. More decisively, the scenario stage is nested inside the domain branch, which stops rendering after step 8. Evidence: [Home.tsx:21](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Home.tsx:21>), [Wizard.tsx:40](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Wizard.tsx:40>), [Wizard.tsx:215](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Wizard.tsx:215>), [Wizard.tsx:342](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Wizard.tsx:342>). **Fix:** separate scenario rendering from domain rendering, enforce track and role prerequisites, then exercise every entry path through results. **Effort: S.**

2. **Official logos and presentation blur independent ownership.** A personal-domain concept using hundreds of official entity marks alongside a DGA-derived design can look sanctioned despite the disclaimer, and directly conflicts with the brief’s “no official logos” rule. The publication status of the supplied workbooks is also not documented in the repository. Evidence: [EntityLogo.tsx:28](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/components/EntityLogo.tsx:28>), [govData.report.md:30](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/data/govData.report.md:30>), [tailwind.config.js:7](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/tailwind.config.js:7>). **Fix:** remove logo assets and references; use the product’s own mark and neutral entity typography. Publish entity-level service records only after confirming they are suitable for public release; otherwise use aggregates. **Effort: M.**

3. **Activity is labeled as impact, and unlike units are added as “benefit.”** Training records and consulting services describe delivery volume, not improved capability or public outcomes. `10,788` is their arithmetic sum, while “Top benefiting entities” implies a valid comparative outcome. The home page calls all 421 served entities governmental although the data distinguishes 370 government or quasi-government entities. Evidence: [Impact.tsx:99](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Impact.tsx:99>), [Impact.tsx:253](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Impact.tsx:253>), [govServices.ts:56](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/data/govServices.ts:56>), [Home.tsx:218](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/Home.tsx:218>). **Fix:** rename this area **سجل خدمات التمكين** (“Enablement service record”), show the two units separately, and segment entity types. Reserve “impact” for measured changes. **Effort: S.**

4. **“Most-demanded skills” are an editorial model presented as observed need.** Consulting service counts are allocated to skill domains with authored weights, then only the top 3 are renormalized to 100%. That percentage is neither the share of all skills nor a workforce-demand measure. The copy also infers a *growing* need from counts without a time trend. Evidence: [sectorSkills.ts:29](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/data/sectorSkills.ts:29>), [sectorSkills.ts:72](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/data/sectorSkills.ts:72>), [GovSectors.tsx:139](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/GovSectors.tsx:139>), [GovSectors.tsx:157](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/src/views/GovSectors.tsx:157>). **Fix:** present “المجالات المرتبطة بمزيج الخدمات الاستشارية” with service counts, denominator, coverage, model version, and **editorial inference** badge. ### C. Idea evolution — proposals for a stronger product

1. **MUST · A capability decision brief.** A Deputy Minister or CIO selects a government mission or service problem and receives a one-page brief: the capabilities involved, current evidence, uncertainty, and 3 decisions to take this quarter. This is more useful than opening separate future-skills, roles, and service-count pages. It needs a small expert-authored mission-to-capability map and claim IDs. **Effort: M. Risk:** causal claims must remain explicit hypotheses.

2. **MUST · Role-to-task maps.** For each of the 24 government roles, show which tasks are changing, which capabilities support them, observable work samples, and the role target used in assessment. This gives HR directors a workshop artifact and employees a reason to choose a role. It needs editorial review of the existing SFIA links and task examples. **Effort: M. Risk:** readers may mistake the mapping for an official SFIA equivalence.

3. **MUST · Evidence cards users can inspect and share.** Clicking a figure or label should reveal its exact source, population, period, definition, confidence, derivation, and last review. Version comparisons should show what changed between releases. This makes the evidence policy visible at the point of use rather than buried in Methodology. It needs a structured claim ledger and source metadata. **Effort: M. Risk:** continuing maintenance.

4. **MUST · Service-record briefs for sectors and eligible entities.** A brief would show service activity by period, comparable denominators, coverage gaps, and cautious capability hypotheses. Deputy Ministers and HR leaders could use it in a planning meeting today. It improves on the current “impact” dashboard by separating observations from interpretation. It needs publication review of entity-level records and a stable snapshot date. **Effort: M. Risk:** disclosure of internal records or unfair comparisons; use aggregate-only briefs if release status is unclear.

5. **SHOULD · Scenario rehearsal followed by a work artifact.** After a judgment question, offer a short debrief and a practical output such as an AI review checklist, service blueprint, or data-classification decision. Employees and managers gain an action they can perform at work, rather than another score. It needs reviewed examples and rubrics for each track. **Effort: M. Risk:** one “best” answer may oversimplify real policy constraints.

6. **SHOULD · Private 90-day workspace and report.** Let the employee choose one or two priorities, mark weekly actions locally, and print a bilingual report containing assumptions, source IDs, and next check-in. It improves on the current fixed 13-week template and basic browser print button. It needs task templates, local schema migration, and print design. **Effort: M. Risk:** a shared device can expose local history; make deletion and private use obvious.

7. **SHOULD · Local team planning workshop.** Participants voluntarily export a minimal, anonymous capability summary; an HR facilitator imports files on one device and sees only cohorts above a minimum size. No server and no individual-result URL are required. This turns the concept into a planning tool for HR directors. It needs an export schema, aggregate rules, and suppression of small groups. **Effort: L. Risk:** re-identification in small teams.

8. **WOW · Intervention comparison board.** A leader compares 2–3 options such as training, peer practice, or specialist hiring against a selected capability gap, with evidence requirements, lead time, and an explicit “unknown” column. It would make the platform a decision aid without inventing ROI. It needs curated intervention playbooks and owner-entered assumptions. **Effort: L. Risk:** false precision if assumptions are rendered as forecasts.

### E. Engineering review for static deployment on omary.me

- **Routing:** Use React Router with paths such as `/ar/skills/:id`, `/ar/sectors/:id`, and matching `/en/...` routes. Links, Back, reload, and share should work for public material. Keep assessment answers and reports in local storage, never in URL parameters. Deploy on a static host with a route fallback; GitHub Pages alone is awkward for clean deep links. Set Vite `base: '/'` for omary.me and test direct entry to every public route.

- **Bundle and loading:** Use `React.lazy` and dynamic `import()` per route. Move the 404 KB entity file and associated detail data into generated static JSON shards or route-specific modules; keep only a compact search index and home summary in the first load. Precompute sector summaries at build time. Remove the 485 official logos under the stated constraint. Set an initial JavaScript target near **180 KB gzip or less**, then inspect the generated chunk report and mobile main-thread time. The current local snapshot is about **416 KB gzip** for JavaScript.

- **SEO and sharing:** Generate crawlable HTML for important public routes at build time, plus per-page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph images made with the product’s own identity, `hreflang`, sitemap, and `robots.txt`. Exclude assessment, results, and private local reports from indexing. The current [index.html](</Volumes/Alomary SSD/01-Projects/DIGITAL-SKILLS-RADAR-20261021/repo/index.html:6>) still describes career guidance as a primary purpose.

- **Accessibility:** Give each route a skip link, one H1, route-change focus handling, and an announced title. Replace clickable SVG `<g>` and `<text>` elements with keyboard-operable adjacent controls and a data table; keep the radar as a visual summary. Use native buttons/links for entity cards, support Enter and Space, and make expanded state and Escape behavior explicit in menus. Verify Arabic and English accessible names rather than fixed English `aria-label` strings.

- **RTL and i18n:** Preserve `lang` and `dir` at document level, but render chart geometry and bidirectional text deliberately. Store taxonomy codes with `ar`/`en` labels instead of printing raw Arabic workbook categories in English. `react-i18next` or an equivalent typed dictionary can handle interface copy and plurals; use `Intl.NumberFormat` with `numberingSystem: 'latn'` and explicit Gregorian date formatting. Add a content check for Eastern Arabic digit characters.

- **Local privacy:** Version and validate stored records, guard storage writes against quota or disabled storage, and provide an obvious “delete all local data” control near the assessment entry and report. Team aggregation, if built, should import files locally and suppress small cohorts.

- **PWA/offline:** A small offline shell and public reference content could help field use. Defer full offline caching until source freshness and update notices exist; never precache the whole directory or stale evidence by default. Workbox documentation specifically cautions against excessive precaching and stale markup. [Chrome Workbox guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/workbox/precaching-dos-and-donts)

- **Testing and release:** Add focused scoring and data-invariant tests, then Playwright journeys for each persona in both languages, direct-route reload, Back, narrow mobile screens, keyboard use, print, and storage-disabled mode. Run `axe-core` and a numeric-claim/source validator in CI. Use a static preview deployment, run `npm ci`, typecheck, tests, build, link/source checks, and bundle budget before an atomic production deploy. The repository currently has only `dev`, `build`, and `preview` scripts and no checked-in deployment workflow.

### H. What NOT to do

- Do not put official entity logos back into the personal-domain concept or imply DGA endorsement through near-identical branding.
- Do not turn service volume into outcome, workforce demand, or a league table of entity performance.
- Do not publish individual assessment results in shareable URLs or send team summaries to a hosted endpoint by default.
- Do not add more careers, technologies, or forecast percentages before existing claims have source, confidence, and review dates.
- Do not label a self-report plus one scenario per domain as validated competence, or use it for selection, promotion, or inter-entity comparison.
- Do not launch a full offline cache that can silently preserve outdated evidence; a small, versioned offline shell is enough.
- Do not spend the 2-day push on a framework rewrite or visual spectacle while the assessment flow, evidence labels, and public routes remain unresolved.
