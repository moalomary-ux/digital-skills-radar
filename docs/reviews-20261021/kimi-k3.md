  # Digital Skills Radar (رادار المهارات الرقمية) — Independent Review, Redevelopment Round 2026-10-21

  Reviewer scope: full read of `src/` (27 source files, 23 data files), `docs/handover/` (HANDOVER-v8, synthesis, 8 review files), build output in `dist/`, and spot-verification of cited numbers (WEF FoJ 2025, SFIA 9, GASTAT) against public sources. No files modified.

  ## A. Verdict

  - **Product concept — 8.5/10.** A skills-radar for government *humans* with a real 682-entity government data layer underneath is a genuinely differentiated, ownable concept — nothing comparable exists in the Saudi GovTech demo space.
  - **User journey — 6/10.** The assessment→results→90-day spine is excellent, but it is buried inside 19 state-switched views with no URLs, a 10-item "أدوات متخصصة" dropdown, and conditional nav items that vanish until you have history (`Header.tsx:35,42-47`).
  - **Visual/brand — 7.5/10.** The green/amber/Plex-Arabic theme is applied with unusual discipline (173 logical-property utilities, zero physical `ml-/mr-` anywhere) and the hand-built SVG charts are a craft signal; it still feels like "very good prototype," not "finished product," mainly because of sharing, print, and first-load weaknesses.
  - **Engineering — 5/10.** One 1.6 MB JS chunk (~406 KB gzip) containing all 1.3 MB of data, no code-splitting, no CI, manual gh-pages deploy, the only test not wired into `package.json`, no router, no SEO surface, keyboard accessibility holes.
  - **Content/evidence — 8/10.** The evidence discipline is real — I verified the WEF 2025 figures (170M/92M, +110% big data, 39% churn) and SFIA-9 codes against primary sources and they check out — but the directory headline overclaims, the ISCO coverage claim is false, and two unsourced/vague numbers survive in flagship copy.

  **Overall: 7/10.** A strong v8 prototype that is one disciplined engineering + data-hygiene pass away from being demoable to a Deputy Minister without apologies.

  ## B. Top 10 weaknesses, ranked by impact on a senior government audience

  **1. No URLs, no back button, nothing shareable.** The #1 usage-killer for this exact audience: senior officials live in WhatsApp/X threads — "look at this page" is how platforms spread. Today every one of the 19 views is the same URL; refresh on an entity detail page dumps you to home; the back button exits the site entirely. Evidence: `src/store.tsx:101` (`useState<View>('home')`), `store.tsx:138-141` (`go()` sets state, scrolls top), `App.tsx:40-58` (conditional render); grep for `pushState|location|hash` in `src/` finds nothing. Fix: hash-based router (`wouter` hash mode or ~60 lines of custom code — static-host safe, no server rewrites), one URL per view, deep-link params for entity/sector/tech/career replacing the five `pendingX` hacks (`store.tsx:165-197`). Effort **M**.

  **2. The "682 government entities" headline is falsifiable by the audience.** `Directory.tsx:63` says "682 جهة حكومية وشبه حكومية" but the data's own `scope` field marks 114 خاصة + 34 أهلية + 1 دولية = 149 entries that are neither. Worse, any official can find: "موسم الرياض", "موسم الرياض 2022", "موسم الرياض 2023" as three separate `type: 'جهة'` entries (`govEntities.ts:9033,9048,9063`); the defunct "الهيئة العامة للرياضة (سابقة)" presented as live (`govEntities.ts:9719`); UNU-EGOV (a Portuguese UN research unit) in the directory (`govEntities.ts:13586`); a field-swap bug where an English company name sits in the Arabic name field with `nameEn: null` (`govEntities.ts:13149`); and a news website ("أخبار 24", `govEntities.ts:8482`). A GM demoing this *will* be asked "why is Riyadh Season 2022 a government entity?" Fix: default the directory to `scope ∈ {حكومية, شبه حكومية}` with an explicit "الكل" toggle; add an `archived` status; fix the swapped record; reword the headline to the true count of government entities. Effort **S**.

  **3. Internal-records provenance will trigger the one question the owner cannot afford.** The impact dashboard's 7,915/2,873/377/421 figures are attributed to "سجلات خدمات هيئة الحكومة الرقمية الرسمية" (`Impact.tsx:319`) but derive from private `inbox/*.xlsx` workbooks with no public URL — while the footer simultaneously says the platform is "غير تابع لأي جهة رسمية" (`Footer.tsx:95`). In a government room this reads as either leaked data or fabricated data; both are bad. Fix: owner decision — either add an explicit permission/provenance line ("بيانات مقدمة من فريق… بغرض العرض المفاهيمي") or relabel as clearly-marked illustrative sample data. Effort **S** (but requires the owner's call).

  **4. First load: one 1.6 MB JS chunk + render-blocking Google Fonts.** Everything — 19 views, framer-motion, and all 1.3 MB of TS data including the 13,629-line `govEntities.ts` — ships in `dist/assets/index-*.js` (~406 KB gzip), eagerly imported at `App.tsx:5-23`; `vite.config.ts` (7 lines) has no `manualChunks`. The fonts stylesheet loads 11 weights of two families synchronously in `<head>` (`index.html:8-13`), and Inter is barely used (`tailwind.config.js:30` stack is Plex-first). On ministry wifi this is a multi-second white screen before the hero. Fix: `React.lazy` per view, dynamic-import the data modules (keeps types, splits for free), self-host subset WOFF2 of Plex Arabic 400/600/700. Effort **M**.

  **5. Zero link-share surface.** One static bilingual `<title>` for all views (`index.html:7`), no OG/Twitter card, no favicon, no canonical, no per-view `document.title` (zero hits in `src/`). Shared on X/WhatsApp — the primary channel for Saudi government comms — the link renders as a bare URL with no title, no image, no description card. For a "flagship concept demonstration" this is a visible defect. Fix: OG/Twitter meta + a designed 1200×630 OG image, favicon, per-view `document.title` in the router effect. Effort **S**.

  **6. Copy and count inconsistencies a minister's staff will catch.** Home promises "مكتبة الأدوار الأربع والعشرين" (`Home.tsx:30`) while the Wizard offers "مكتبة الأدوار (20 دورًا)" (`Wizard.tsx:173`) — the data actually has 24 roles (verified count in `roleLibrary.ts`). `Methodology.tsx:21` asks "كيف صُنّفت المهن الثلاثون؟" while coverage is 50 careers (`Methodology.tsx:44` itself says 50). `Methodology.tsx:105` claims approximate ISCO codes "لكل منها" but `careerIsco` (`careers.ts:2534-2565`) covers 30 of 50. Fix: single source of truth for counts (derive from data, don't hardcode), correct the ISCO claim to "30 مهنة". Effort **S**.

  **7. Keyboard accessibility is broken on an Arabic-first government platform.** Clickable entity rows are `div`s with `role="button"` and an Enter handler but **no `tabIndex` anywhere in the codebase** (`Directory.tsx:120-129`, `GovSectors.tsx:192`), so they are unreachable by keyboard; the radar chart's `onAxisClick` sits on SVG `<text>` with no keyboard equivalent (`RadarChart.tsx:258`); aria-labels are hard-coded English ("skills radar chart", "close") on an Arabic-first site; there is no skip link. Government procurement checklists in Saudi increasingly reference WCAG. Fix: real `<button>`s or `tabIndex={0}`+key handlers, Arabic/English aria via `t()`, skip link, and a visually-hidden data table mirroring each chart. Effort **M**.

  **8. Two numbers violate the platform's own "لا ننشر رقمًا بلا مصدر" rule.** The AI-cluster essay claims an emerging-tech adoption index "تجاوز 76٪" with no source (`technologies.ts:78-79` — the figure is real, DGA 2026 = 76.04%, but unsourced on-page), and `careers.ts:1096` cites "65% من مختصي الموارد البشرية… وفق استطلاعات القطاع" — unnamed surveys, exactly the pattern v6 purged. Separately, the Saudi demand signal for AI/data rests on a single commercial source (Coursera 2025: `careers.ts:451`, `futureSkills.ts:35`), a fragility the review committee already flagged and which remains unmitigated. Fix: source both figures, add a second Saudi signal (e.g. GASTAT ICT survey, LinkedIn/jobs-platform aggregate) or hedge explicitly. Effort **S**.

  **9. The high-stakes disclaimer arrives too late in the journey.** The "أداة تأمل وتخطيط تطويري" notice (`Disclaimer.tsx:20`) first appears at Results (`Results.tsx:219`) — after the user has answered 40 statements, 8 scenarios and 24 knowledge checks in the Wizard, which carries no usage-boundary notice at all. For an audience primed to ask "can we use this for promotions?", the boundary must be stated at the start of the assessment, not after it. Fix: one honest line on Wizard step 0. Effort **S**.

  **10. No CI, manual deploy, unwired test — the omary.me cutover is currently fragile.** Deploy is `npm run build` + manual push to `gh-pages` (per `HANDOVER-v8.md:122`); there is no `.github/workflows/`; `scripts/levels.selftest.mjs` (24 boundary assertions) is not referenced by any `package.json` script; `vite.config.ts:6` hard-codes `base: '/digital-skills-radar/'` and no `CNAME` exists, so the custom domain would 404 assets today. Fix: GitHub Action (tsc → selftest → build → deploy gh-pages), `public/CNAME` with `omary.me`, `base: '/'`. Effort **S**.

  ## C. Idea evolution — proposals that materially strengthen the product

  **C1. Shareable state URLs, including a shareable results snapshot — MUST.** Extend the router (B1) so an assessment *summary* (role, track, 8 domain scores, stage — never raw answers) encodes into a compact URL-safe token on the results page: "شارك نتيجتك". Static-safe, PDPL-clean, and turns every user into a distribution channel. Serves everyone; it is the difference between a demo and a product people send each other. Data: none new. Effort **M** (depends on router). Risk: keep the token to aggregates only; document that in the privacy note.

  **C2. Printable bilingual "ملف الجهة المهاري" — entity skills brief — WOW.** Turn `EntityDetail` into a designed one-page A4 brief (print CSS → PDF): logo, classification, training/consulting history chart, computed top-5 skill demands from `sectorSkills`, recommended audience track, source + disclaimer. An HR director or the GM can walk into a meeting with a physical artifact — that is how government concepts travel. Data: all existing (`govEntityDetails`, `sectorSkills`). Effort **M**. Risk: print-stylesheet polish is finicky; accept 2-page max.

  **C3. Client-side team aggregation via share codes — WOW.** A manager taps "قارن فريقك", gets a short code. Each member finishes the assessment and copies their *aggregate* code (same encoding as C1). The manager's browser merges N codes into a team radar — mean/min/max per domain, count, no names, all local. Zero backend, zero data collection, and it answers the question every CIO/HR director actually asks: "where does my team stand?" Serves CIO, HR director, team leads. Data: none new. Effort **M–L**. Risk: must be explained with absolute clarity that codes contain only aggregates; cap team size; no persistence beyond localStorage.

  **C4. Role-to-role mobility map — SHOULD.** The platform already stores 24 roles × 8 domain target vectors (`roleLibrary.ts`). Compute pairwise domain-distance at build time and show, on each role card and in Results: "من محلل أداء إلى محلل بيانات: فجوة في مجالين" — a government-internal career mobility map. Nobody else has this for Saudi government roles; it converts the role library from reference data into guidance. Serves mid-level employees and HR planners. Data: existing vectors + ~50 lines of computed similarity. Effort **S–M**. Risk: keep distances descriptive ("المجالات المشتركة") not prescriptive.

  **C5. Executive view "نظرة قيادية" — MUST.** One curated page for the Deputy Minister persona: national enablement KPIs, a sector × skill-demand heatmap (from `sectorSkills`), top growing/declining skills — three visuals, each with source + date, each screenshot-worthy, each printable. Today these exist scattered across Impact/GovSectors/FutureSkills; a senior official will not assemble them himself. Data: existing. Effort **M**. Risk: resist adding more than ~5 blocks; curation is the feature.

  **C6. Proper PDF report for individual results — MUST.** Replace bare `window.print()` (`Results.tsx:195`) with a dedicated print stylesheet producing a branded 2-page report: radar, stage, evidence-agreement flags, top-3 gaps, 90-day plan, methodology note, disclaimers. An employee can put this in front of their manager; a training team can collect them. Effort **S–M**. Risk: none material; print CSS is deterministic.

  **C7. Practice mode: scenarios as learning content — SHOULD.** The 40 judgment scenarios are currently consumed once during assessment. After results, offer "تدرّب على المواقف" for weak domains: re-run the scenario set with rationales as *teaching* material, decoupled from scoring. This converts the most expensive content asset (1,255 lines of authored scenarios) into an ongoing learning loop. Data: 2–3 additional scenario variants per domain would help but v1 can reuse existing. Effort **M**. Risk: clearly separate practice scores from assessment results to protect the evidence model.

  **C8. Sector skill-demand briefs as citable tables — SHOULD.** Each of the 31 sectors gets a compact "ملف الطلب على المهارات" page: the computed profile, the underlying consulting-category counts it was derived from, the editorial-weight caveat, and a date. This transforms `sectorSkills.ts` from an internal computation into a *citable evidence artifact* — the thing a studies team can reference in a memo. Data: existing. Effort **S–M**. Risk: the category→domain weights are editorial; the page must say so in the same breath (it currently does, in `Methodology.tsx`).

  **C9. Annual, dated "حالة المهارات الرقمية في الحكومة" report page — WOW.** A curated, versioned report (2026 edition) combining the enablement data, futures signals and methodology — the citable centerpiece that gives omary.me a reason to be referenced, and a natural annual update ritual that signals the platform is alive. Serves the owner's public thought-leadership. Data: synthesis of existing + editorial writing. Effort **L**. Risk: editorial maintenance commitment; do not start it unless the owner will date-stamp and update it.

  Deliberately not proposed as priorities: Saudi wage/vacancy integration (correct, but blocked on data agreements — keep the "قيد العمل" honesty), and psychometric validation (correctly deferred to a national program; the disclaimers already carry this).

  ## D. Information architecture and journey

  Current: 7 main-nav items + a 10-item hover dropdown + conditional items = 17 destinations competing in the header (`Header.tsx:20-51`). Proposed: **6 top-level items**, hubs with in-page tabs.

  1. **الرئيسية** (Home) — keep, but fix the role-count copy (B6).
  2. **قيّم مهاراتك** — the assessment spine: `wizard → results → pathways`. Show "النتائج" and "خطة التطوير" *always* in this section's sub-nav (not conditionally as in `Header.tsx:35`), with the existing honest empty states ("لا توجد نتائج بعد — ابدأ التقييم") when `history` is empty. This is the product's core loop and should never hide.
  3. **المهن والمستقبل** — one hub, four tabs: `Careers` (المهن الخمسون), `Decide` (اختبار التوجيه — keep its counselor links), `FutureSkills` (مهارات المستقبل 2026–2030), and `Lists` demoted to a tab (ما يزدهر وما يندثر) — it is 167 lines of WEF tables, not a destination. `Explore` (الخريطة التفاعلية) becomes a fifth tab here; it visualizes exactly this hub's data.
  4. **الجهات والقطاعات** — one hub landing on the `Entities` guidance page, with tabs: `Impact` (أثر التمكين), `GovSectors` (القطاعات), `Directory` (دليل الجهات), and `EntityDetail` reachable only in-context (as today). If C5 is adopted, "نظرة قيادية" lands here as the default tab for this hub.
  5. **المكتبة المرجعية** — tabs: `Roles` (الأدوار الوظيفية), `SkillRegistry` (سجل المهارات), `TechRadar` (رادار التقنيات), `Standards` (المعايير والتقارير).
  6. **المنهجية والشفافية** — `Methodology` stays top-level and *visible*. For this audience, a prominent transparency page is a trust feature, not documentation debt.

  Net effect: 19 views → 6 destinations with 4–5 tabs each; nothing removed, only re-shelved. Arabic naming above is deliberate: verbs for the user journey (قيّم), nouns for reference (المكتبة المرجعية), and "الشفافية" paired with "المنهجية" to signal posture.

  Primary journeys per persona:

  - **Deputy Minister:** الرئيسية → الجهات والقطاعات → (نظرة قيادية / أثر التمكين) → print/share. Two clicks to the numbers.
  - **CIO:** المكتبة المرجعية (رادار التقنيات، الأدوار) → الجهات والقطاعات (القطاعات → ملف الطلب على المهارات).
  - **HR director:** الجهات والقطاعات (دليل الجهات → ملف الجهة المهاري, C2) → قيّم مهاراتك (team aggregation, C3).
  - **Mid-level employee:** قيّم مهاراتك → النتائج → خطة التطوير (90 يوم) → المهن والمستقبل (mobility map, C4).

  ## E. Engineering review for a static deployment on omary.me

  **Routing / deep links / back button.** Adopt a **hash router** — either `wouter`'s hash mode (~2 KB) or a ~60-line custom `useHashRoute` hook. Hash routing is the right call for GitHub Pages/custom-domain static hosting: no server rewrite rules, no 404.html gymnastics, and URLs like `omary.me/#/entity/ent-0075` are fully shareable and crawlable-enough for this use. Map: `#/`, `#/assess`, `#/results`, `#/plan`, `#/careers`, `#/careers/:id`, `#/sectors`, `#/directory`, `#/entity/:id`, `#/library/:tab`, `#/methodology`. The five `pendingX` deep-link hacks in `store.tsx:165-197` become URL params and the store shrinks accordingly. Every view sets `document.title` (bilingual) in an effect.

  **Bundle size and code-splitting.** Today: one 1,628 KB chunk (`dist/assets/index-gLuaTt7U.js`, ~406 KB gzip), of which ~1.3 MB is data. Plan: (1) `React.lazy` + `Suspense` for all non-home views in `App.tsx`; (2) convert the heavy data modules (`govEntities.ts` 404 KB, `careers.ts` 280 KB, `scenarios.ts`, `technologies.ts`) to `await import()` at the views that need them — this keeps TypeScript typing and Vite splits automatically, simpler and safer than a JSON-fetch refactor; (3) `manualChunks: { vendor: ['framer-motion'] }` so the animation lib caches separately. Target budget: **initial JS ≤ 250 KB gzip, each lazy route ≤ 120 KB gzip**. Measure with `vite build --mode analyze` (rollup-plugin-visualizer) in CI.

  **Logos.** 485 PNGs, 6.3 MB, 150×150 px, unoptimized. Add a build script (sharp or `squoosh-cli`) converting to WebP at 150px — expect <1 MB total — keep PNG fallback only if you must support very old Safari; otherwise WebP-only with the existing monogram fallback (`EntityLogo.tsx:7-36`). Lazy loading is already correct.

  **SEO / meta / sharing.** Full `<head>` pass on `index.html`: OG + Twitter cards with a designed 1200×630 image, favicon + apple-touch-icon, canonical, `theme-color #1B8354`. Honest note: as a client-rendered SPA, per-page indexing is limited; if search visibility for the reference content matters later, evaluate `vite-plugin-sitemap` + prerendering just the 3–4 static pages (home, methodology, standards) via `vite-plugin-prerender` — but do not SSR the whole app; the cost/benefit is wrong for a concept demo. The critical win is link-share rendering (OG), not Google ranking.

  **Accessibility.** (a) All clickable rows/cards become real `<button>` or `<a>` elements (fixes focus, Enter/Space, and screen-reader announcement in one move); (b) every SVG chart gets a visually-hidden `<table>` twin — this is both the keyboard and the screen-reader solution for radar data, better than bolting ARIA onto SVG; (c) bilingualize aria-labels via `t()`; (d) add a skip link; (e) run `@axe-core/playwright` scans in CI (see testing). The existing `:focus-visible` and `prefers-reduced-motion` work (`index.css:95-116`) is good — keep it.

  **RTL correctness.** Already the strongest part of the codebase: 173 logical-property utilities, zero physical direction classes, `<html dir>` flip in `store.tsx:123-124`, mirrored chart winding in `RadarChart.tsx:82`, Western digits enforced via `nu-latn` (`Results.tsx:118`). The only task left is a regression guard: a lint rule or CI grep that fails the build on `ml-|mr-|text-left|text-right` in `src/`.

  **Performance budget.** Initial JS ≤ 250 KB gzip; initial CSS ≤ 15 KB; fonts: self-host 3 weights of IBM Plex Sans Arabic as WOFF2 subsets (drop Inter, or keep one weight if English headings need it); LCP ≤ 2.5 s on simulated Fast 3G; enforce via Lighthouse CI in the pipeline with a fixed budget file. Kill the 11-weight synchronous Google Fonts request (`index.html:10-13`).

  **PWA / offline.** Yes — `vite-plugin-pwa` (Workbox under the hood), app-shell precache + runtime cache for logos. Offline capability is a real differentiator for government field demos (basements, conference wifi) and costs almost nothing on a static app. Add `manifest.webmanifest` with the product's own icon and name in both languages. Do **not** cache anything user-specific — there is nothing user-specific server-side anyway.

  **i18n.** Keep the current `{ar, en}` pair approach — at 416 inline `t()` calls plus typed data records it is simple, type-safe, and grep-able; `react-i18next` would add weight without solving a problem you have. One improvement: extract *view-level* UI strings into per-view `strings.ts` dictionaries so a translator can review Arabic without touching JSX; data files keep inline pairs as today.

  **Testing strategy for a static site.** (1) Wire `scripts/levels.selftest.mjs` into `npm test` today — it exists and passes 24 assertions but nothing runs it. (2) Add Vitest for the pure logic: `computeScores` (`store.tsx:247`), `computeAgreement` (`Results.tsx:35-40`), `recommendCareers`, scenario shuffle determinism (`Wizard.tsx:18-28`), `sectorSkills` computation. These are the functions a reviewer can falsify; they're all pure and testable in an afternoon. (3) Playwright smoke: boot the built site, visit every route, tab through one full keyboard pass, run axe-core per route — this is what protects the a11y work from regressing. (4) Lighthouse CI budget as above. No component-snapshot tests; they rot faster than they help here.

  **Build/deploy pipeline.** GitHub Actions on push to main: `tsc --noEmit` → `npm test` (selftest + vitest) → `vite build` → deploy `dist/` to gh-pages. Add `public/CNAME` containing `omary.me`, set `base: '/'` in `vite.config.ts`, keep `.nojekyll`. DNS: A records to GitHub Pages IPs + CNAME file, enforce HTTPS in repo settings. The whole pipeline is ~40 lines of YAML and eliminates the current manual-push fragility (`HANDOVER-v8.md:122`).

  ## F. Content and evidence review

  What would embarrass the owner, with locations — roughly in severity order:

  ## H. What NOT to do

  - **Do not add a backend, accounts, or login.** The no-backend posture is the product's privacy story and its PDPL answer in one sentence; C3 proves the team-analytics itch can be scratched client-side. A backend would add hosting cost, a security surface, and a data-governance review for near-zero audience gain.
  - **Do not invent benchmarks or percentiles.** With no population data, any "أنت في المئين 63" is fabricated precision — the exact sin the labor economist flagged. C4/C5 use only computed-from-real-data comparisons.
  - **Do not adopt a heavy stack** (Next.js/SSR, Redux/Zustand, a chart library, react-i18next). The hand-built SVG charts are a differentiator, the store is 255 lines and adequate, and every added dependency is supply-chain and bundle weight on a static site.
  - **Do not use official DGA branding, logos, or endorsement language** — the v3.1 decision to stay "DGA-inspired, never DGA" is what keeps omary.me safe; the internal-records question (B3) already sits near this line, don't push further.
  - **Do not add gamification** (badges, streaks, leaderboards). It clashes with the senior-government register, trivializes the evidence layers, and implicitly encourages the competitive high-stakes use the platform explicitly bans.
  - **Do not embed an AI chatbot.** A client-side chatbot needs an API key in the browser (leak + cost abuse) or a backend (see above), and hallucinated career advice to Saudi citizens is a liability no disclaimer will cover.
  - **Do not add analytics trackers.** "We collect nothing" is a feature; a third-party script would contradict the privacy claim on the first network-inspection by any technically literate official.
  - **Do not scale career coverage before fixing Saudi grounding.** The committee consensus ("don't expand the base before tightening it") still stands: 50 well-sourced careers beat 200 thin ones, and the open P1-5 (wages/vacancies) is the correct next content investment when data agreements exist.
  - **Do not claim psychometric validity or add reverse-scored "trap" items** without a validation program; the current honest framing ("تحريرية ولم تُختبر ميدانيًا", `scenarios.ts:12-14`) is the defensible position — half-measures would be worse than none.
  - **Do not fetch live external data at runtime** (scraping GASTAT/job boards client-side): CORS-fragile, legally gray, and breaks the versioned, reviewable data pipeline that makes the methodology page credible. Keep data editorial, dated, and shipped with the build.
