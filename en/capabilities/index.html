Roles & skills

A shared language for development: 8 domains, five skills in each, and one scale of 4 levels. A role sets the target level in each domain; the role category sets what you are expected to show.

AI & Data

Level: Application

You apply the skill in routine tasks with limited guidance.

Reading, analyzing and governing data, and using generative AI responsibly in government work.

Its five skills: Data literacy, Data governance, Data analysis & visualization, Generative AI use, Responsible & ethical AI

The target level is not the same for everyone: it depends on the role, and it is a development guide, not a job requirement.
