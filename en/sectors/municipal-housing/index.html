How to read this brief

The figures here are service records, not a measure of workforce need: they describe what the sector’s entities requested in training and consulting up to the cut-off, not what each entity needs. The category-to-domain link is a declared, reviewable editorial assumption, and a sector whose entities with records fall below the rule has no published aggregates, so that no entity can be identified from a small total.
