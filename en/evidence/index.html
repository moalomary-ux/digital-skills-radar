Method & sources

Evidence register

Our rule: no number without a source. Every figure in the tool is registered here as a claim with its sentence, value, scope and period, a source with title and publisher, the source type, a confidence level and the last review date. Wherever the figure appears in the tool, its badge opens the same details.

Source type is not confidence: an official record is strong evidence of what was recorded and weak evidence of a need that was not. A claim is flagged as needing review once 12 months have passed since its last review.

Source types and confidence levels

Source types

  • Official source25A record or statistic from an official body; it may be published or an internal administrative record (stated explicitly).
  • International report or framework47A report or reference framework from an international organization or standards body.
  • Survey3A result from a defined survey sample; it describes respondents, not everyone.
  • Self-reported data7A figure an organization publishes about itself or its platform; not independently verified.
  • Editorial45A judgment or rule set by the tool’s author; declared and reviewable, not a measurement.

Confidence levels

  • High confidenceCan be relied on as stated, within its scope and period.
  • Medium confidenceCorrect as far as we know, but it carries a declared limit or has not yet been matched to the original text.
  • Low confidenceAn assumption or weakly supported figure; indicative only, not a basis for decisions.
