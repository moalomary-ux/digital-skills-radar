Skills dictionary

The registry holds 40 skills in 8 domains, and each skill has its own record: definition, the role categories that need it, an example task, the evidence basis, prerequisites and the linked self-assessment statement.

Editorial records, not yet field-tested; the evidence basis describes the frameworks for the skill family, not the single skill.
